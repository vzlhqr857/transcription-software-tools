## FAQ

**How accurate are AI transcription services compared to human transcription?**

Top AI transcription tools now reach 95-99% accuracy on clean audio with clear speech, making them suitable for most use cases including meetings, interviews, and content creation. Human transcription still wins for complex scenarios with heavy accents, multiple overlapping speakers, poor audio quality, or specialized terminology where context matters—professional transcribers deliver 99-100% accuracy but cost more and take longer. For everyday needs, AI transcription followed by quick human review provides the best balance of speed, cost, and accuracy.

**What's the difference between transcription and subtitle generation tools?**

Transcription converts spoken words into text documents for reading, searching, and documentation purposes, focusing on accuracy and speaker identification rather than visual display. Subtitle generation creates time-synced captions designed to appear on screen during video playback, emphasizing proper timing, readability, and formatting that matches platform requirements. Many modern platforms do both—transcribe first, then convert transcripts into properly timed subtitle files with styling options for burned-in captions or separate SRT/VTT files.

**Can transcription tools handle multiple speakers and languages in one recording?**

Most quality transcription platforms automatically identify and label different speakers through diarization technology, separating "Speaker 1" and "Speaker 2" without training. You can rename speakers during editing and changes apply throughout the transcript automatically. Language mixing within single recordings proves trickier—most tools transcribe in one primary language at a time, though some platforms support translation of finished transcripts into 40+ languages after initial transcription completes. For recordings genuinely mixing languages mid-conversation, professional human transcription handles complexity better than automated systems.
